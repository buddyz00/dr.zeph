STORM PROOF Relationships

Building Relationships That Can Weather Life's Storms

Relationships are among the greatest sources of meaning, connection, and strength in our lives. They can also be where we experience some of our greatest challenges.

I created STORM PROOF Relationships to translate what I have learned through marriage and family therapy, research, ministry, teaching, and my own life into practical tools that help people build stronger, healthier, and more resilient relationships.

The 4Ds

At the heart of STORM PROOF is a simple framework I call the 4Ds:

  1. 01Defaults
  2. 02Differences
  3. 03Distress
  4. 04Deliberateness

We all enter relationships with defaults—patterns of thinking, feeling, communicating, and responding that have developed throughout our lives. When our defaults encounter the differences of another person, those differences can sometimes produce distress. STORM PROOF helps people learn to move toward deliberateness—developing the awareness and skills to respond to themselves and others with greater intention.

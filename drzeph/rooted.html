ROOTED International Institute for Family & Community Mental Health

Strengthening Relationships. Expanding Access. Helping Communities Flourish.

Mental health support should not depend solely on whether a community has enough mental health professionals.

ROOTED International Institute for Family & Community Mental Health is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization committed to strengthening the capacity of communities to support mental health, relationships, and resilience.

We work with organizations and frontline workers to help bring practical mental health knowledge and resources into the places where people already live, learn, work, worship, and receive care.

OUR VISION

A world where communities flourish through strong relationships and accessible mental health support.

OUR MISSION

ROOTED International Institute for Family & Community Mental Health strengthens community mental health by providing Resilience Oriented Outreach, Training, Education and Development to organizations and frontline workers.

Our Name

What ROOTED Means

Our name reflects our approach and the four areas through which we pursue our mission:

R.O.

Resilience Oriented

Grounding our work in approaches that build upon the strengths, relationships, and resources of individuals and communities to strengthen their capacity to navigate adversity and promote well-being.

O.

Outreach

Developing and implementing ROOTED-led initiatives that bring practical mental health and resilience resources directly into communities.

T.

Training

Partnering with organizations to equip frontline workers and others with practical knowledge and skills for supporting mental health, relationships, and resilience.

E.

Education

Creating accessible tools, resources, and educational materials that organizations and communities can use to strengthen mental and relational health.

D.

Development

Supporting the development and capacity of organizations and community-based initiatives working to strengthen mental health and well-being.

Our Approach

Rooted in Communities

We believe sustainable change happens when communities are equipped to build upon their own strengths, relationships, knowledge, and resources.

Through partnerships in the United States and internationally, ROOTED International Institute for Family & Community Mental Health works to expand access to practical mental health support while strengthening the people and organizations already serving their communities.
